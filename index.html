About Almost Café

Almost Café is a cozy, underground hangout in Lajpat Nagar, Kanpur. We offer a relaxed vibe, pocket-friendly prices and an eclectic menu — from comfort breakfasts to luscious shakes and pizzas. Perfect for friends, families and casual dates. Our space is designed to be calm and intimate with music that complements conversation, not drowns it out.

  • Timings: 9:00 AM — 12:00 AM
  • Price Range: ₹200–₹400 per person
  • Services: Dine-In · Drive-Through · No-contact delivery
